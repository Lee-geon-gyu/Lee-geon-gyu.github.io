# Asset provenance

Portfolio descriptions, profile information, project artwork, logos, links and original fonts were preserved from Lee Geon-gyu's original portfolio. `public/content-integrity.json` records the original image hashes.

## Scanned resources

The source manifest is `public/assets/asset-sources.json`. Downloaded source files retain their original URLs and hashes there; large source materials/models were relocated to the ignored `tools/source-assets` folder. Runtime derivatives are `connected-atelier.glb`, `potted-02.glb` and `tree-small.glb`.

## Generated resources

Architecture and furniture are generated by the project's Blender scripts. Static sunlight and bounced light are calculated in Cycles with the same scanned foliage used in the browser. `rooted-garden.glb` derives its leaves and photographic UVs from the CC0 scanned Tree small 02 asset. Original connected trunks, branching tendrils, balcony runners and soil-rooted bed planting are generated in Blender from `garden-layout.json`. The same world-aligned derivative is used for browser shadows and the Cycles bake. Afternoon sky, rooftop panorama, jute, book spines and ivory marble are Image Gen raster assets made for the approved afternoon art direction. The original images and exact prompts are preserved in the workspace `outputs/production-assets` folder.

## Software and example resources

- Three.js and its official Water, Reflector, postprocessing modules and water-normal example texture: MIT. A copy of the Three.js license is distributed at `public/licenses/three-MIT.txt`.
- Google Draco decoder: Apache-2.0. Decoder files are taken unchanged from Three.js's glTF decoder bundle. Upstream documentation is preserved at `public/assets/draco/README.md` and the original license is [Google Draco LICENSE](https://github.com/google/draco/blob/main/LICENSE).
- React, Vite, three-mesh-bvh and lucide-react retain their package licenses. Icons use the existing Lucide library.

Foliage light transmission mask: public/assets/foliage-light-mask.png, generated with the built-in Image Gen tool, then copied into this project. The asset filters real-time spotlight illumination; it is not a scene/background photograph. Exact prompt saved in tools/foliage-light-mask-prompt.txt.

Pool caustic luminance mask: public/assets/pool-caustics.png, generated with the built-in Image Gen tool and copied unchanged. The live water shader bends and moves this light pattern while retaining camera-dependent reflections and water normals. Exact prompt saved in tools/pool-caustics-prompt.txt.

Original afternoon piano BGM: `public/assets/afternoon-piano.wav`, composed and synthesized specifically for this portfolio by `tools/compose_afternoon.py`. A 90-second seamless, slow felt-piano and soft-pad arrangement; no third-party recordings, samples, or melodies are used. Playback is optional, low-volume, and fetched only when the listener turns on BGM.
